# Mandatory human review and escalation

Return cannot_determine or a conditional draft and escalate; do not make a definitive recommendation when any of these apply:

- taxpayer facts conflict or are not evidenced;
- place of establishment/fixed establishment or customer establishment is unclear;
- service category, human intervention, or mixed/composite supply is uncertain;
- conflicting country evidence or uncertain business/customer status;
- a live VAT-ID/VIES check is needed, unavailable, or stale;
- transaction is at/near a tax threshold or year boundary;
- tax point, subscription period, advance payment or continuing-supply date is uncertain;
- refund, correction, cancellation, retroactive adjustment or credit note is involved;
- unusual cross-border structure, non-EU use/enjoyment, or another Member State obligation may apply;
- credible primary sources conflict, law is not version-verified, or material interpretation is unsettled;
- provider behavior or invoice wording is material and current official documentation was not verified.

Escalation must name the reason category, evidence needed and appropriate reviewer (accountant, tax adviser, legal counsel, or human reviewer). Human review is not a rubber stamp. Preserve the draft and provenance. No filing, invoicing, tax-status selection or production setting change follows from a draft conclusion.
