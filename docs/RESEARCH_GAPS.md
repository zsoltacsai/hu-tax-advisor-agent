# Research gaps backlog

This is a research backlog, not legal conclusions. Each item requires authoritative source review and qualified Hungarian tax adviser/accountant confirmation before executable rules are proposed.

- Current, versioned Hungarian VAT Act (Áfa tv.) text and amendment/transitional history remain unverified in this repository.
- Hungarian continuous-supply, subscription, advance-payment, and tax-point rules.
- AAM eligibility, turnover computation, election, exceptions, and transitional rules beyond the arithmetic threshold.
- EU SME scheme applicability to Hungarian taxpayers, thresholds by Member State, notification/EX workflows, and interaction with AAM/OSS.
- Reverse-charge liability, invoice references/wording, and Hungarian invoicing implications.
- Community summary statement implications and customer-status evidence.
- OSS thresholds, election, registration, tax point, and interaction with the SME scheme.
- Electronic-services classification, including the exact WP CareGrid service; no classification has been made.
- Supplier/customer fixed-establishment analysis and which establishment receives the service.
- Refunds, partial refunds, corrections, cancellation, credit notes, and retroactive status changes.
- Foreign VAT registration, local exceptions, use-and-enjoyment rules, and non-EU obligations.
- Article 59c conditions and national implementation for any proposed Article 58 workflow.
