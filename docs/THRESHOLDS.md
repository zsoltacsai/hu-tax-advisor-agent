# Threshold component

The generic threshold comparator accepts a known numeric amount and a source-backed threshold record. It reports below_threshold, at_threshold, above_threshold, or unknown; it does not aggregate production turnover or determine legal eligibility.

The 2026 Hungarian AAM record uses NAV official guidance for HUF 20,000,000 and references Áfa tv. 188. § (2) and 377. §. NAV describes historical/current expected turnover conditions and transitional rules. The comparator is arithmetic only: it cannot establish that the taxpayer elected AAM, satisfies all eligibility conditions, or may apply an exemption to a given cross-border transaction. Those questions always escalate.
