## Fresh-source findings and limits

- EUR-Lex official consolidated Directive 2006/112/EC dated 2025-04-14 was accessed. Article 196's condition is not simply “Article 44 result”: the recipient must fit the stated taxable-person / VAT-identified legal-person category and the supplier must not be established in the taxing Member State, subject to relevant rules such as Article 192a. Articles 262(1)(c), 263 and 264 add separate recapitulative-statement scope, timing and data conditions. This is EU legislation text, not a complete destination-state implementation review.
- NAV's 2026 26A60 page and completion guidance were accessed; the page lists a version dated 2026-09-02. It describes service-supply reporting on 26A60-03, but an available form/instruction does not independently establish a particular supplier's filing duty.
- Hungarian VAT Act NJT full-text page was accessed at the current URL and its header displayed a consolidated version date of 2025-12-20. A consolidated-version date is not the effective-from date of every provision. No global effective-from value should be inferred from it.
- Current NAV 29. information booklet (2026) is an administrative secondary-to-statute source. It does not replace transaction-specific source-version checks or a review of the applicable statutory provisions.
- No source access in this review constitutes VIES lookup, live customer validation, destination-state law review, or accountant approval.

The structural pipeline is deterministic, not an independent AI legal reviewer. All five readiness results are therefore `BLOCKED`; there is no human approval candidate to present in this pass.
