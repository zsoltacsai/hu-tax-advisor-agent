# Invoice treatment metadata

Research records EU Directive Articles 219a and 226(11a), Hungarian Áfa tv. §§159(2)(c) and 169(n), and NAV 2026 guidance. The legal texts support a reverse-charge mention where the customer is liable, subject to conditions and applicable invoice rules. The Phase 5 candidates describe metadata only; they do not produce a rendered invoice, language-specific wording, rate, or complete field list.

`invoice_treatment` remains `not_assessed` until explicit human approval and full fact checks. Do not infer invoice behavior from a provider, payment method, or Article 44 result alone.
