# WP CareGrid service-classification research questionnaire

**Status: unanswered. No service classification is assigned in Phase 4.** Do not inspect the WP CareGrid repository, billing, customer records, or production behavior. Obtain a reviewed service description from the responsible human owner and have a tax adviser assess it.

For each customer plan/contract, answer with evidence and dates:

1. What exactly does the customer purchase, and what deliverables are promised?
2. Which tasks are performed automatically, and which require staff action?
3. Do people actively inspect or review customer websites? At what cadence and under what triggers?
4. Are updates automatic, customer-approved, or individually approved by staff?
5. How are backups initiated, monitored, restored, and communicated?
6. Is security monitoring continuous and automated, or does a person review alerts?
7. Who performs malware investigation/remediation, and how much judgment or customization is involved?
8. What support is included; is it standardized, synchronous, or individually tailored?
9. Can staff make custom code/configuration changes or other interventions?
10. How often does a human intervene per customer and what work is done?
11. Could the service operate and deliver its promised output without human involvement?
12. Are outputs standardized or individually performed for each customer?
13. Are hosting, software access, licensing, consultancy, security, backups, and maintenance bundled? Can customers buy them separately?
14. What do contracts, marketing, onboarding, and actual operational practice say, and do they differ?
15. What changed over the subscription period; are service elements economically distinct or potentially composite?

The future reviewer may consider `GENERAL_SERVICE`, `ELECTRONICALLY_SUPPLIED_SERVICE`, `HUMAN_PERFORMED_TECHNICAL_SERVICE`, `MIXED_OR_COMPOSITE_SERVICE`, or `OTHER`. Internet delivery, SaaS branding, and a product name alone do not determine classification. Record missing facts and escalate unresolved legal classification.
