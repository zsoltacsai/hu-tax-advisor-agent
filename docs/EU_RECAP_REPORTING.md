# EU recapitulative statement and Hungarian A60

Directive Article 262(1)(c) addresses specified services for which the recipient is liable under Article 196 and that are not exempt in the Member State of taxation. Article 264 describes VAT identification data. NAV 2026 A60 guidance describes qualifying Article 37(1) services in A60-03, while A60 versioning and submission periods are mutable.

The candidates `eu-recap-services-reporting-candidate@1` and `hu-a60-services-reporting-candidate@1` are non-executable. Article 44 alone is not enough to infer a reporting duty. Customer/supplier VAT identifiers, destination taxability, taxpayer reporting cadence, corrections and period rules remain subject to current verification. No filing or submission automation exists.
